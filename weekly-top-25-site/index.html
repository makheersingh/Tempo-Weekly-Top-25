How the 25 are ranked

35% raw 7-day gain · 25% 7-day % growth · 15% share of the gain in the last 3 days · 15% share of days up over 30 · 10% Tempo momentum score.

Each input is converted to a 0–100 percentile across all eligible artists first, so one huge percentage cannot swamp the rest.

Groups

Breakout: +100% or more in 7 days and at least 15,000 listeners gained.

Accelerating: mid-size artists (30,000 to 100,000 listeners) not already in Breakout.

Scale: 100,000+ listeners.

Early signal: under 30,000 listeners.

Selection

Each Thursday's export is split into three lists of 25, dealt in ranked order within each group so every list carries the same group mix and a similar average score. Accounts that are not artist signals, such as religious recitations, are removed before ranking.
